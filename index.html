THE POSITIONS

WHAT THE JOBS DO

👑

Class President

Enforces the classroom rules and leads the class.

⭐

Class Vice President

Helps the President enforce the classroom rules and takes over the President's job when the President is gone.

🛡️

Sheriff

Reports people breaking the classroom rules to the teacher.

⚖️

Class Judge

Decides whether someone is guilty or innocent after the Sheriff reports a possible classroom rule violation.

CLASSROOM SYSTEM

HOW IT WORKS

01

A Rule Is Reported

If the Sheriff hears about someone breaking a classroom rule, the Sheriff reports it to the teacher.

02

The Judge Reviews It

The Judge considers the reported situation and decides whether the person is guilty or innocent.

03

The Class Is Led

The President leads the class and enforces the classroom rules, while the Vice President helps.
