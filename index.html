THE POSITIONS

WHAT THE JOBS DO

👑

Class President

Enforces the classroom rules and leads the class.

⭐

Class Vice President

Helps the President enforce the classroom rules. If the President is gone, the Vice President takes over the President's job.

🛡️

Sheriff

Reports people who are breaking classroom rules to the teacher.

⚖️

Class Judge

Decides whether someone is guilty or innocent after the Sheriff reports a possible rule violation.

CLASSROOM SYSTEM

HOW IT WORKS

01

Sheriff Reports

If the Sheriff hears about someone breaking a classroom rule, the Sheriff reports it to the teacher.

02

Judge Decides

The Judge reviews the reported situation and decides whether the person is guilty or innocent.

03

President Leads

The President leads the class and enforces the classroom rules, with help from the Vice President.
